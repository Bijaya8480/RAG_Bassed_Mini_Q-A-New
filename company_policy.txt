Company Policy Document

Annual Paid Leave
All full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of annual paid leave per calendar year. Part-time employees receive pro-rated leave based on their working hours. Leave requests must be submitted at least 3 working days in advance through the HR portal. Emergency leave may be granted with same-day notification to the direct manager.

Working Hours
Standard working hours are Monday to Friday, 9:30 AM to 6:00 PM. Employees are expected to be available during core hours from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM. Flexible start times between 8:30 AM and 10:00 AM are permitted with manager approval.

Lunch Break
All employees are entitled to a 1-hour unpaid lunch break. The break must be taken between 12:00 PM and 2:00 PM. Employees may split the break into two 30-minute periods with supervisor approval.

Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week with prior manager approval. Remote work requests must be submitted at least 1 week in advance. Employees working remotely must be available during core hours and attend all scheduled meetings via video conference. The company provides a one-time home office stipend of $500 for eligible employees.

Lost Equipment
Any lost or damaged company equipment must be reported to IT immediately. Employees are responsible for the replacement cost of lost equipment due to negligence. IT will provide a replacement within 2 business days for critical equipment (laptops, phones). A police report is required for stolen equipment valued over $1000.

Business Travel Expenses
All business travel expenses must be submitted within 15 calendar days of trip completion. Receipts are required for all expenses over $25. Per diem rates: $75/day for domestic travel, $100/day for international travel. Airfare must be booked at economy class unless flight exceeds 6 hours. Hotel expenses are capped at $200/night for domestic and $300/night for international travel.
